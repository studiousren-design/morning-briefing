Sources are disagreeing again this morning, so here's the honest picture rather than a single confident number.

- The Met Office's regional feed instead pointed to a warmer day — around **22–23°C**, with cloudy conditions and outbreaks of light rain in the morning, turning drier in the afternoon with some brighter spells and isolated showers. A separate Met Office reading for the London City Airport area gave a much lower **10% chance of rain at 6pm**.

What's consistent across sources, though, is that **some rain — whether in the morning or afternoon — is a real possibility today, not a long shot**. Given that, **bring your umbrella today**, and dress in layers since the temperature outlook is genuinely uncertain.

**NVIDIA (NVDA)** — Still riding the momentum from its fiscal Q2 2027 report on August 26th: revenue of **$96.2 billion**, up **106% year-over-year**, with non-GAAP EPS of **$2.22** — well ahead of the $2.09 analysts expected. Mark your calendar: the next quarterly dividend of **$0.25/share** has an ex-date of **September 10th**.

**Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM)** — Continuing to ramp advanced 3-nanometer production amid strong AI-chip demand, and reportedly agreed to produce three new chips for Xiaomi, including a next-generation 3nm smartphone processor. TSM raised its 2026 revenue growth outlook to **"slightly above 40%"** and increased capex guidance. Analyst sentiment remains a **"Strong Buy"** consensus with a 12-month price target around **$554**. Note for your calendar: TSM's dividend has an ex-date of **September 16th** — the same day as the next Fed decision.

**Marvell Technology (MRVL)** — A genuinely volatile stretch. Marvell reported record Q2 fiscal 2027 revenue of **$2.74 billion**, up 37% year-over-year — a strong beat — but shares have fallen roughly **14–15% over the past week** (down about 4.8% most recently) on investor concern that a major cloud deal with Google won't contribute meaningfully until fiscal 2029. **Qualcomm (QCOM)** — A striking recovery story: shares are up roughly **15% over the past month**, trading around **$170**, on optimism about non-handset growth — particularly data centers and automotive, where Qualcomm posted a record $1.6 billion in automotive revenue last quarter (up 61% year-over-year). QCOM's dividend has an ex-date of **September 3rd — tomorrow**.

AAPL has gained roughly 36.5% over the past year and 15.7% year-to-date, trading largely flat into the event.

**Palantir (PLTR)** — Trading around **$182–183** after a huge run: shares are up over **43% in the past month** following a blowout Q2 report (revenue up 93% year-over-year to $1.94 billion, commercial revenue more than doubling). Markets fell sharply to start September: on Tuesday, September 1st, the **S&P 500 dropped to 7,631.47**, the **Dow lost 419 points to 52,766.88**, and the **Nasdaq Composite fell 271 points (about 1%) to 26,099.77**.
