## SEGMENT 5 — THE BIGGER PICTURE: US MARKET & MACRO

### Where Things Stand Heading Into Today

Wednesday, September 23rd was a rough session: the **Dow Jones fell 352.10 points (-0.68%) to 51,511.59**, the **S&P 500 dropped 0.75% to 7,706.03**, and the **Nasdaq Composite tumbled 1.13% to 26,936.04**. The proximate cause was a sharp spike in Treasury yields (below). Futures heading into today's open were reported as **little changed** — Dow futures down roughly 15 points, S&P and Nasdaq futures only marginally lower — suggesting markets are pausing rather than extending Wednesday's selloff, at least as of the last data available.

### Story One: Treasury Yields Hit a 19-Year High

The **10-year Treasury yield surged to around 5.10–5.11%**, its **highest level since July 2007**, in its biggest one-day move in nearly 18 months. Drivers cited: **stronger-than-expected US economic activity surveys** (notably manufacturing), **hawkish commentary from a senior Federal Reserve official**, **weak demand at a 5-year Treasury note auction**, and **persistently high oil prices** feeding inflation concerns. This is the dominant story pressuring equities and is worth watching closely — higher yields raise borrowing costs economy-wide and make bonds more competitive with stocks.

### Story Two: Oil Prices and the Ongoing US–Iran Conflict

**Brent crude rallied 3.86% to settle at $103.08/barrel; WTI crude gained 1.81% to $92.16/barrel.** Oil has risen more than 18% this month, driven by the ongoing **US–Iran conflict and strikes in the Strait of Hormuz**, a corridor that normally carries roughly a fifth of the world's oil supply — shipping traffic through it has reportedly collapsed by around 95%. Forecasters are sharply split on where this goes: some project Brent above $140 with recession risk, while others (e.g., Goldman Sachs) still see a base case of oil easing back toward $85. This is a genuinely live, high-uncertainty situation — treat any single forecast with caution.

### Story Three: The Trump–Xi Summit — Today Is the Main Event

**Today, September 24th, is the core day of Xi Jinping's state visit**: Trump formally welcomes Xi and Peng Liyuan at the White House, followed by a **Rose Garden guard-of-honor ceremony**, the **main summit meeting**, and a **state dinner** tonight. Reported outcomes so far: an **extendable one-year deal to keep rare-earth mineral supplies flowing** (Trump called this "settled... for the world"), a **10-percentage-point cut to the fentanyl-related tariff** (from 20% down toward 10%), and a commitment from China to make **large soybean and farm-product purchases**. Notably for your portfolio: **semiconductor export controls reportedly were not part of the preliminary US agenda**, though an **AI dialogue/hotline** between the two governments may be set up. This is a more incremental outcome than a full chip-policy breakthrough — relevant context for NVDA and QCOM given their China exposure.

### Story Four: Jobless Claims Due Today

Weekly **Initial Jobless Claims** for the week ending September 19th are due for release today; I could not confirm the actual printed number as of this briefing. For context, the prior week (ending September 12th) showed claims falling to **196,000**, well below the ~208,000 consensus — a labor market that has stayed resilient so far. Today's number will feed directly into the market's read on whether the Fed hikes again, given the hawkish tone already driving yields higher.
