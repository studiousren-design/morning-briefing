### Story One: Bank of Japan Hikes Rates to a 31-Year High

Overnight, the **Bank of Japan raised its policy rate by 25 basis points to 1.25%**, the highest level since 1995, in a 7-2 vote. The BOJ cited the risk that inflation will overshoot its 2% target, and this hike came just **three months after its last one** — a faster pace than the roughly six-month gaps seen previously. A more hawkish BOJ typically strengthens the yen and can ripple into global risk sentiment and Treasury yields, which is relevant to your whole portfolio, not just one name.

### Story Two: Today Is Quarterly "Triple Witching"

Today, September 18th, is a **quarterly triple witching day** — stock options, index options, and index futures contracts all expire simultaneously. This falls just two days after the Fed's rate hike and on the same day as the BOJ decision, which raises the odds of **higher-than-normal volatility and trading volume** today, independent of any specific company news.

### Story Three: Oil Eases Slightly, Hormuz Tensions Persist
