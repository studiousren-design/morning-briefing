**Xi Jinping's state visit to Washington runs roughly September 23rd–25th**, with the **core Trump–Xi meeting at the White House on Thursday, September 24th** — now just two days away. This is described as the single biggest upcoming catalyst for **NVDA**, given NVIDIA's guidance currently assumes zero China data-center chip revenue (see Segment 3), and for **QCOM** given its own China exposure.

**WTI crude is trading around $93.18/barrel and Brent around $101.51/barrel today** — both down from last week's elevated levels (Brent was around $103.68 as of Monday's briefing), and this pullback in oil is part of what supported Monday's stock rally.
