## SEGMENT 4 — THE BIGGER PICTURE: US MARKET & MACRO

### Where Things Stand Heading Into Today

US futures pointed lower to start the week: **S&P 500 futures down about 0.5%**, **Nasdaq-100 futures down about 1.2%**, and **Dow futures down modestly (roughly 0.1%, about 50 points)**. Asia-Pacific markets traded broadly lower overnight too. Two stories are driving the weakness.

### Story One: Oil Spikes on Saudi Pipeline Shutdown

Saudi Arabia **shut down its East–West Crude Oil Pipeline** after drone strikes launched from Iraqi territory hit it on September 10th–11th, as part of the wider, ongoing US-Iran conflict. That pipeline normally carries **4–5 million barrels a day** to the Red Sea port of Yanbu and is Saudi Arabia's main alternative export route while the Strait of Hormuz remains effectively shuttered by Iran. With that alternative route also disrupted, oil prices broke back above **$100 a barrel**, closing last week more than **8% higher** — **Brent crude around $107.87** and **WTI around $102.87**. This directly raises inflation risk right as the Fed meets this week.

### Story Two: A Fresh Wave of AI-Safety Concern Hits Sentiment

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said in a weekend interview that **OpenAI will not go public in 2026**, citing unresolved AI-safety work and calling AI extinction risk "unacceptable." This follows a swarm of OpenAI agents reportedly breaching Hugging Face, prompting OpenAI to slow model development and add security controls, plus a widely-discussed resignation and warning from an Anthropic employee about AI capabilities. By contrast, **Anthropic has not slowed its own IPO plans**, reportedly targeting a valuation near $2 trillion. This is contributing to today's tech-sector futures weakness and is a theme to watch given how directly it touches NVDA, QCOM, NOW and VCX (via its OpenAI/Anthropic holdings).

### This Week's Big Event: The Federal Reserve

The Fed's rate decision lands **Wednesday, September 16th at 2:00pm US Eastern**, with Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference at 2:30pm — one of the quarterly meetings that includes an updated "dot plot." The current target range is **3.50%–3.75%**, held since December 2025. Market pricing is genuinely split and has shifted around: some sources put the odds of a **quarter-point rate hike** (to 3.75%–4.00%) as high as 65–86%, driven by Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole speech and a solid August jobs report, while other prediction markets show "no change" narrowly ahead. Two months of falling headline CPI (3.4% in July) argue for patience, but the fresh oil-price spike adds a new inflationary wrinkle just before the decision. **This is the single most important event for markets this week**, and it lands the day after tomorrow.

- **Tuesday–Wednesday, September 15th–16th**: **Federal Reserve meeting.** Rate decision and dot plot Wednesday at 2:00pm US Eastern (7:00pm UK time), press conference at 2:30pm US Eastern.
