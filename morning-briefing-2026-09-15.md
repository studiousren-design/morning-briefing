## SEGMENT 4 — THE BIGGER PICTURE: US MARKET & MACRO

### Where Things Stand Heading Into Today

US stocks fell Monday, pulled down by a combination of **surging oil prices, weakness in AI-linked stocks, and escalating Middle East tensions** — the Dow closed down about 184 points, the Nasdaq down about 469 points, the S&P 500 down about 48 points, and the small-cap Russell 2000 down about 14 points. The 10-year Treasury yield also hit its highest level since 2023. All of this comes right as the Federal Reserve's two-day policy meeting begins today.

### Story One: Oil Stays Elevated on Middle East Escalation

Saudi Arabia's **East–West Crude Oil Pipeline remains offline** following last week's drone strikes, with Saudi Aramco yet to say how long the disruption will last. Adding to the tension, **Iran has claimed a tanker was hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz**, a further escalation in the same conflict. West Texas Intermediate is trading near **$102 a barrel** and Brent above **$105**, keeping inflation risk elevated right as the Fed meets.

### Story Two: AI-Safety Debate Escalates — Now With a Possible Industry Response

This has moved well beyond OpenAI's IPO delay. Reports indicate **Anthropic, OpenAI and Google DeepMind have been holding working-group meetings since July aimed at creating an industry-led AI safety standards body**, with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei driving the push and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman backing it as an alternative to waiting on government regulation. Amodei published a roughly 3,800-word essay over the weekend arguing that addressing AI risk "requires ... pacing the rate of capabilities advancement so that risk prevention has time to keep up." On the political side, **President Trump publicly rejected calls for an AI slowdown**, saying the US intends to keep leading China in AI development. Meanwhile, Anthropic's own IPO plans reportedly remain on track despite the safety debate, while OpenAI's are delayed into next year. This is a live, fast-moving story with direct relevance to NVDA, QCOM, NOW and VCX (via its OpenAI and Anthropic stakes).

### This Week's Big Event: The Federal Reserve

The Fed's two-day meeting runs **today (Tuesday) through tomorrow, September 16th**, with the rate decision and updated "dot plot" due Wednesday at 2:00pm US Eastern (7:00pm UK time), followed by Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference at 2:30pm Eastern. Market pricing has shifted notably more hawkish since yesterday: one source now puts the odds of a **quarter-point rate hike** (to 3.75%–4.00%) at roughly **90%**, up from the 65–86% range reported over the weekend — driven by hotter-than-expected August core CPI, a strong nonfarm payrolls report, and now the fresh oil-price spike adding further inflationary pressure. Notably, some analysts expect stocks could actually **rally on a rate hike** this week, since one is now so widely anticipated. This remains the single most important event for markets this week.
