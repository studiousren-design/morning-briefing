### Where Things Stand Heading Into Today

The **10-year Treasury yield stands at about 5.25%**, and the **30-year is around 5.57%** — both near their **highest levels in roughly two decades** (the 30-year specifically flagged as a ~24-year high). This continues to be the single biggest headwind for equity valuations, particularly rate-sensitive growth and software names.

The standoff continues: as of Monday, **Brent crude was around $105.48/barrel and WTI around $93.12/barrel**, both elevated on the unresolved dispute after **Trump rejected Iran's conditional offer** to reopen the Strait. A CNN analysis published today argues **Iran has "lost considerable leverage"** in the standoff and can't sustain the current posture indefinitely — a potentially de-escalatory signal worth watching, though nothing is resolved yet. Expect continued oil-price volatility on any headline.

### Story Three: Government Shutdown Risk — Resolved, Correcting Yesterday's Note

Good news to correct the record from recent days: **the shutdown risk at today's 30 September fiscal year-end has already been resolved.** Congress passed, and the President signed (on 2 September), a **continuing resolution funding the government through 11 December 2026**. So there is **no shutdown today** — that headline risk is off the table until the next funding deadline in December, when the 12 annual appropriations bills (or another stopgap) will need to be agreed.

### Story Four: Meta's Enterprise AI Push — Still Rippling Through Software Stocks

Monday's launch of Meta's **"Enterprise Platform"** — an AI stack aimed at corporate customers — continues to pressure enterprise software names, ServiceNow among them (Segment 4). Watch for further read-through commentary today as more software companies respond to the competitive threat.

### Story Five: Key US Economic Data This Week

- **Tomorrow (Thursday, 1 October):** **August PCE / Core PCE** — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, released before the opening bell.
- **Friday, 2 October:** **September jobs report** (non-farm payrolls and unemployment rate) — the week's single biggest potential market mover. Note: some forecaster figures I found for this report looked unusually high and inconsistent with prior trends — **treat the exact consensus numbers with caution and check a live source** before relying on them.

### Other Things Worth Watching

- **Treasury yields** are the dominant macro story right now — watch for any pullback as a signal of eased pressure on equities.
