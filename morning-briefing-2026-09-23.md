### Story One: Xi Jinping's State Visit Begins Today

**Xi Jinping arrives in Washington today, September 23rd**, landing at Andrews Air Force Base for the start of a state visit running through **September 25th** — his first visit to the White House in 11 years. Today is expected to bring **arrival and welcoming ceremony** proceedings; the **core Trump–Xi meeting at the White House, a Rose Garden guard-of-honor review, and a state dinner** are all set for **tomorrow, Thursday, September 24th**. Confirmed agenda items include **AI, chip export policy, rare earth minerals access, US auto market access**, and extending the "Busan" trade truce, with critical minerals and Taiwan also expected to come up. This remains the single biggest near-term catalyst for **NVDA** and **QCOM** given their China exposure.

### Story Two: The Fed's First Hike Since 2023

On **September 16th**, the Federal Reserve **raised its target rate 25 basis points to 3.75%–4%**, its first hike since July 2023, in a unanimous 12-0 vote. Fed Chair **Kevin Warsh** — handpicked by President Trump — said inflation has been "too high ... for too long," citing a stronger-than-expected labor market alongside inflation still running above the Fed's 2% target. Updated Fed projections show **16 of 18 officials expecting at least one more hike this year**, a hawkish signal still working its way through markets and a factor supporting gold (GLDM).

### Story Three: Business Activity Loses Some Momentum

Today's flash **S&P Global Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI)** readings — an early snapshot of how businesses are performing this month — showed **Manufacturing easing to 52** (from 53 in August) and **Services slipping to 53.9** (from 54.5), pulling the **Composite PMI down to 53.6** (from 54.6). Readings above 50 still indicate expansion, but the trend points to the private sector's growth cooling slightly rather than reversing.
