## SEGMENT 4 — THE BIGGER PICTURE: US MARKET & MACRO

### Where Things Stand Heading Into Today

Markets are stabilizing after yesterday's Fed-driven selloff. The **S&P 500 rose about 0.59%** today to roughly **7,596**, recovering part of Wednesday's losses, when the **Dow fell 631 points (1.21%)** to 51,461.90, the **S&P 500 dropped 0.45%** to 7,551.81, and the **Nasdaq slipped a marginal 0.01%** to 25,978.42. ### Story One: The Fed Hiked Rates Yesterday — First Since 2023

The Federal Reserve raised its benchmark rate by **25 basis points to 3.75%–4.00%** yesterday, in a unanimous 12-0 vote — the **first hike since 2023**. The committee cited persistently elevated inflation, with 2026 PCE projected at 3.7% headline and 3.4% core, driven by energy-price shocks, tariffs, and heavy AI-related capital spending. Notably, the Fed's updated dot plot shows **16 of 18 officials expect at least one more hike this year**, with four expecting two more — meaning today's market calm shouldn't be read as the rate-hike story being over. This remains highly relevant to rate-sensitive names in your portfolio, including QS and NOW.

### Story Two: Oil Stays Volatile as Saudi Pipeline Remains Down

**Brent crude fell 2.7% to $105.83/barrel** today, though it's still up more than 16% this month amid escalating conflict in the Persian Gulf. Saudi Arabia's East–West pipeline remains shut after last week's drone attacks; in response, **Saudi Aramco has sold around 20 million barrels to Asian refiners** for pickup near the Strait of Hormuz, rerouting exports via the Red Sea while the US and Iran continue to contest control of Hormuz. At least **two vessels have been attacked in the Strait since Saturday**. The US military is reportedly assisting Saudi Arabia in loading four supertankers (8 million barrels total) at Ras Tanura and Juaymah. This remains a live inflation and volatility risk with direct relevance to GLDM (gold) and the broader market.

### Story Three: Xi Jinping's White House Visit and US-China AI Talks — September 24th

A major date to watch: **Xi Jinping is due to visit the White House on September 24th**, timed around the UN General Assembly in New York — the second in-person Trump-Xi meeting of 2026. Ahead of that, the **first official US-China AI dialogue** under this administration is expected this month, led by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, covering how to regulate powerful frontier AI models. Separately, **combined US tariff exposure on Chinese semiconductors is reportedly approaching 70%** (Section 301), with a new 7.5% "overcapacity" tariff layer reportedly being prepared ahead of the summit. This is directly relevant to NVDA and QCOM, both of which have meaningful China exposure.
