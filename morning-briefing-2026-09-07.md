## SEGMENT 5 — THE BIGGER PICTURE: US MARKET & MACRO

**Markets are closed today for Labor Day.** Here's what happened in the last session before the holiday, and what's ahead this week when trading resumes tomorrow.

### Friday's Session (September 4th) — Hot Jobs Report Rattles Markets

The **August jobs report** landed well above expectations: nonfarm payrolls grew by **+162,000**, far exceeding the roughly +53,000 consensus estimate. That "hot" print increased expectations that the Federal Reserve could raise rates rather than hold or cut, and stocks fell on the news: the **Dow dropped 271.86 points (-0.51%)** to close at 53,414.25, the **S&P 500 slid 0.38%** to 7,718.60, and the **Nasdaq Composite fell 0.29%** to 26,506.99. Losers were led by megacaps — Apple (-2.55%), Alphabet (-2.10%), Microsoft (-2.05%) — while Caterpillar (+1.65%), Honeywell (+0.95%), and Home Depot (+0.88%) outperformed.

### The Fed: A Genuine Coin-Flip Rate Decision

This is the dominant macro story right now. Fed Chair **Kevin Warsh's** hawkish speech at Jackson Hole significantly raised the odds of a **rate hike** (not a cut) at the Fed's next meeting on **September 15–16th** — odds that had been around 30% pre-speech have since been reported anywhere from roughly 48% to over 60% depending on the source, effectively a coin-flip. Warsh has flagged that the Fed's preferred inflation gauge sits at 3.7%, nearly double the 2% target, and Friday's strong jobs number adds further fuel to the hike case.

### This Week's Key Data — Watch Thursday and Friday

With markets closed today, the week's real catalysts land later:
- **Thursday, September 10th**: Producer Price Index (PPI) report, before the opening bell.
- **Friday, September 11th**: Consumer Price Index (CPI) report, before the opening bell — likely the single biggest data point ahead of the Fed meeting. As of early-September tracking estimates, headline CPI was running around 3.38% year-over-year and core CPI around 2.38% year-over-year, but the official Friday print is what markets will react to.
- **Wednesday, September 10th (after close)**: **Oracle (ORCL)** reports fiscal Q1 2027 earnings — a closely watched read on AI infrastructure spending given Oracle's exposure to large AI compute buildouts (including OpenAI-related commitments). Consensus expects revenue of about $19.13 billion and EPS of $1.30.
- **September 15–16th**: FOMC meeting and rate decision — genuinely uncertain between a hold and a hike given the data above.

### Other Things Worth Watching
- **NVDA dividend ex-date**: September 10th.
- **AMAT**: raised 2026 semiconductor systems growth outlook to 30%+, strongly bullish analyst backdrop.
- **QS Q3 earnings**: around October 22nd.
- **Gold (GLDM)**: near multi-month highs on dovish Fed commentary, but that could reverse quickly if the CPI print supports the hike case.
- **VCX**: still the most volatile name on your list by a wide margin — a genuine rate-hike surprise on the 15th–16th could move risk sentiment broadly and hit a name this volatile hard in either direction.
- Broader context: the S&P 500 is up roughly 13% year-to-date, but September is historically the weakest month for US stocks, and investors have been bracing for a pullback even before this week's data.
